# Small project, measured launch

## Market read (September 23, 2026)

Recent Pump.fun tech tokens with traction have a short, demonstrable premise: [familiars.family](https://pump.fun/coin/2PENPmfgJfq6CG3k4byj4oWwHf8SerqakmYHMkUupump) offered AI agents; [Untaxed App](https://pump.fun/coin/3tzkdxJ1qVCaPAuWmd8FjRFKUbwMYpLUAq5gg4Vopump) pitched low-fee trading. Both were being discussed in Pump.fun's live feed on September 23. The lesson is to ship something people can try and share before asking them to buy a token. A visual concept alone is less compelling than a working product with a clear social loop. This is an inference from recent listings, not a forecast. Review current activity on [Pump.fun](https://pump.fun/) before any token decision.

## Product

**MintFaceoff:** “Put two Solana tokens head to head and share the live result.” The first version is intentionally small: paste two mints, inspect market data, share a link or image, or draft a challenge on X. It requires no wallet, paid service, or custom domain. Market data comes from the [DEX Screener API](https://docs.dexscreener.com/api/reference).

## Distribution experiment

1. Share 10 useful faceoffs on X with a specific question for each community; link the product, without claiming a winner predicts returns.
2. Invite community moderators and creators to make their own matchups. Ask which metric or format they would actually share.
3. Measure unique visitors, completed comparisons, shared links/cards, repeat use, and community posts. Add privacy-conscious analytics only after choosing a provider and publishing a notice.
4. Improve the one feature people ask for most. Keep the app fast on phones.

## Token decision gate

The coin concept, if the experiment works, is the public faceoff community around the app. **MINTFO** is a tentative ticker; it is not reserved, and a final on-chain and brand collision check is needed before launch. Consider a coin only after the product earns repeat organic use and community interest without token incentives. Before creating it, write a clear token purpose and risk disclosure, confirm the relevant platform rules, and set a public launch policy. A $1M market cap is a target, not a predictable outcome. No token has been created, offered, or promoted by this project.
